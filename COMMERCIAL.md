# Commercial model (day 1)

OutreachSkill is **MIT open source**. The CLI, free `outbound-basics` skill, and
pack stubs are free forever.

## What is free
- `outreach` CLI (`list`, `draft`)
- `skills/outbound-basics/` bilingual starter templates
- Pack stubs under `packs/*/BUY_URL` so agents and humans can find paid downloads

## What is paid
Industry template packs are **paid digital downloads** fulfilled by
[Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com):

| Pack | Checkout |
| --- | --- |
| SaaS Outbound | https://checkout.dodopayments.com/buy/pdt_0NqiQ1DjJ2hEPigqc8JHE?quantity=1 |
| Indie Hacker | https://checkout.dodopayments.com/buy/pdt_0NqiQ1c3DGg6do30ONeZa?quantity=1 |

Paid zips are **not** vendored in this repository. Buyers get the full template
libraries after checkout; the OSS tree only advertises the links.

## Why this split
Ship a useful free surface on day 1 (Show HN / agents can run `npx outreach`),
while monetizing high-effort bilingual industry copy as one-time downloads.
Future options (not required to use the free CLI): cloud usage credits, more
vertical packs, localized expansions.
