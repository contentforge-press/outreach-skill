# Outbound Basics — bilingual starter draft

Use this free pack to send a respectful first-touch note. Swap bracketed fields.
Keep one clear ask. Do not attach decks on first contact.

---

## English

**Subject options**
1. Quick idea for {{company}}'s {{pain_area}}
2. {{mutual_context}} — thought of your team
3. 3-line note on {{outcome}} (no deck)

**First touch**

Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed {{specific_observation}} at {{company}}. Teams in a similar spot often
lose time on {{pain_area}}.

We built {{product}} so {{role_plural}} can {{outcome}} without {{old_way}}.
{{one_proof_metric}}.

Would a 15-minute call next week be useful, or should I send a 1-pager instead?

— {{your_name}}
{{your_title}} · {{your_company}}
{{calendar_or_email}}

**Follow-up (day 4–5)**

Hi {{first_name}} — bumping this once. Happy to share how {{peer_company}} cut
{{metric}} with {{product}}. No worries if the timing is off — just say the word.

## 中文

**主题备选**
1. 关于 {{company}} 在 {{pain_area}} 上的一个小想法
2. {{mutual_context}} — 想到你们团队
3. 三行说明：如何提升 {{outcome}}（不附材料）

**首封**

{{last_name}}{{title_honorific}} 你好：

注意到 {{company}} 最近在 {{specific_observation}}。同类团队常在 {{pain_area}}
上耗掉不少时间。

我们做了 {{product}}，帮助 {{role_plural}} 在不必 {{old_way}} 的情况下达成
{{outcome}}。{{one_proof_metric}}。

下周方便聊 15 分钟吗？或者我先发一页说明也可以。

— {{your_name}}
{{your_title}} · {{your_company}}
{{calendar_or_email}}

**跟进（第 4–5 天）**

再打扰一次：若时机合适，我可以分享 {{peer_company}} 用 {{product}} 把
{{metric}} 做下来的做法。若现在不合适，直接说一声即可，谢谢。

---

## Field cheat-sheet / 字段速查

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `{{company}}` | Prospect company |
| `{{first_name}}` / `{{last_name}}` | Contact name |
| `{{pain_area}}` | Concrete pain (billing, onboarding, SEO…) |
| `{{product}}` | Your product name |
| `{{outcome}}` | Result you sell (faster close, fewer tickets…) |
| `{{one_proof_metric}}` | One credible number or named proof |
| `{{peer_company}}` | Similar customer (only if allowed) |
